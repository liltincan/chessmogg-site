2. What the app does
ChessMogg analyzes publicly available chess games. You provide a Chess.com username; the app fetches that account's public games from Chess.com's public API and computes statistics on your device. Opening population statistics and puzzles are read from bundled databases derived from Lichess's public CC0 exports. The Coach sends the current chess position to Lichess for cached Stockfish evaluation and sends your question, position, game moves, and evaluation evidence to OpenAI to generate a reply.
